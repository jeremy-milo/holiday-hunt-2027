Congratulations!

You solved all twelve puzzles! But you didn\'t think claiming the treasure would be quite that simple, did you?!

Hidden in each of the twelve puzzle pages is a secret word. First you must gather all twelve secret words, being careful to keep them in their exact order. These are the twelve \"seed words\" that protect the Bitcoin wallet where the treasure was stashed.

Once you have the twelve seed words, download a Bitcoin wallet app (like BlueWallet). Tap \"Add a wallet\", choose the standard Bitcoin wallet type, then tap \"Import wallet\" instead of the \"Create\" button. Now type the twelve seed words in their correct order into the box, and then tap \"Import\".

At that point, the treasure chest is unlocked but you haven\'t taken the treasure into your posession. You still need to send the treasure to a new Bitcoin wallet that only you control. If you don\'t act fast, someone else might snatch the treasure first!

So open or create a different Bitcoin wallet (not the treasure wallet), get your wallet\'s receiving address, and send everything from the treasure wallet to your address. Now at long last, the treasure is yours!!!
